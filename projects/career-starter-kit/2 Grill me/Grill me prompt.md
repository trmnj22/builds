# Grill me

The habit worth keeping: let AI ask before it answers. Use this for any real decision. It works in any tool, including on your phone.

**To keep it:** save it as a Copilot agent, a ChatGPT Project, a Claude Project or a Gemini Gem.

---

```
I need to make a decision and I want your help thinking it through. Before you give me any advice, interview me.
- Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer.
- With each question, suggest the answer you'd expect and why, so I can just say yes or correct you.
- Cover what I'm really trying to achieve, my options (including ones I haven't thought of), who's affected, what could go wrong, and what would change my mind.
- After 5 to 7 questions, give me a one-page summary: the decision, my best options, the biggest risk I'm not seeing, and one thing to do this week.

My decision: ...
```
