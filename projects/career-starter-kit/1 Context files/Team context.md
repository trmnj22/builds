# Team context

**Who writes it:** the team's leader. This is the leader's job, not something to delegate.
**Who uses it:** everyone in the team, under the company context and above their own "About me" file.
**Length:** one page.

The leader sets the **why** and the **boundaries**. Each person then works out the **how** in their own file. That's job design: you give people the goal and the guardrails, and trust them with the detail.

> **Before you fill this in:** check your organisation's AI policy. Keep it to what you'd say in a team meeting. No confidential numbers, no names of clients or staff.

**Faster way:** paste this whole file into your AI tool and say: *"Interview me to fill this in for my team. One question at a time, and suggest the answer you'd expect so I can say yes or correct you."*

---

## Our purpose
*Why this team exists, in one or two sentences. What would break if we disappeared tomorrow?*

## Who we serve
*Our customers, internal or external, and what they need from us.*

## This year's goals
*Three at most. Each one measurable.*
- Example: cut the time to approve a new supplier from six weeks to two.

## How we decide
*What each person can decide alone, what needs the team, and what needs me.*

## What good looks like
*How we'd know a piece of work is great, not just done.*

## Guardrails
*What we never do, and when to stop and ask.*
- Example: anything that commits money over $10K goes to me first.

## Roles in the team
*One line per role (not per person): what it owns and what it doesn't. Each person details theirs in their own "About me" file.*
