# Fit Rubric

The scout scores every job the same way, so scores can be compared from day to day. You can change the weights. Keep them adding up to 100.

The fit % is a sorting aid, not a prediction of getting the job.

Never score on age, gender, family status, ethnicity, religion, disability or anything similar, whether it is about you or written in the ad. Flag wording such as "digital native" or "young team" as "age-coded wording".

Only full ads are scored. Leads (a title and a link, or a page the scout could not read) are not scored.

## Step 1. Deal-breakers (checked first)

Check the ad against `Preferences.md`: Deal-breakers, Industries to avoid, Locations, Work mode, Work type, the pay floor (only if the ad states pay) and Clearances or licences I hold.

If any deal-breaker is hit, cap the total at 40% and name the deal-breaker under Flags. Still show the job.

## Step 2. Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| A. Must-haves | 35 | See A below |
| B. Seniority and scope | 20 | See B below |
| C. Industry and function | 15 | 1 same industry or function, 0.5 adjacent or transferable, 0 unrelated |
| D. Location, work mode and work type | 10 | See D below |
| E. Pay | 10 | 1 at or above the target in Preferences, 0.5 within 10% below target, 0 further below |
| F. Nice-to-haves and depth | 10 | 1 most desirable criteria met and backed by several notes, 0.5 some met or one note only, 0 none |

### A. Must-haves
- Take the 3 to 6 essential criteria from the ad.
- Score each one: 1 = Strong (a career note proves it with a stated result), 0.5 = Partial (shown without a result, or a close match), 0 = Gap (no note shows it).
- A = 35 x the average.
- Every point has a wikilink to the note that proves it. A Skill note marked `Claimed only` counts as Partial at most.
- If an open Gap note covers the requirement, link it.

### B. Seniority and scope
Seniority steps, lowest to highest: Team leader, Manager, Senior manager, Head of or Director, General manager or Executive, C-level. Judge by team size, budget, who the role reports to and title, in that order, using only what the Roles notes say.
- Same level: 1
- One step up: 1
- Two steps up: 0.5
- One step down: 0.5, and add the flag "One step down, check you want this"
- Two or more steps down: 0

### D. Location, work mode and work type
- 1 when location, work mode and work type all match Preferences.
- 0.5 when one of them is workable but not what Preferences asks for.
- 0 when one of them conflicts.
- A fixed-term or contract job when Preferences says permanent only scores D at 0 and is flagged. If work type is listed as a deal-breaker, the 40% cap applies too.

### C and E notes
- If the employer is not named and the ad is vague, score C as 0.5 and write "unclear".
- If the ad does not state pay, or the target in Preferences is blank, leave E out and work the score over the remaining weights. Write "E not used, no target set" or "E not used, no pay in ad".

## Step 3. The total

Total = (sum of score x weight) / (sum of weights used) x 100, rounded to a whole %.

Then apply the caps, lowest cap wins:
- Any deal-breaker hit: at most 40%.
- Evidence floor: if A is under 12 of 35, at most 60%, confidence Low, and add the note "Mostly fits on level and location, not on what the job asks for".

## Step 4. Confidence

Shown next to the score, never mixed into it.
- High: full ad read and must-haves clear. Missing pay in the ad lowers it to Medium. A blank Target in Preferences does not.
- Medium: full ad read, but the ad states no pay or the must-haves are unclear.
- Low: search result text only, or under the evidence floor. Show a range of 7.5 points either side, rounded to whole numbers, for example "53 to 68%".

## Step 5. Show the working

On the shortlist, every scored job shows `Score: A n/35, B n/20, C n/15, D n/10, E n/10, F n/10`. Write "E not used, no target set" or "E not used, no pay in ad" when E was left out.
