# About Inbox

Put your own files here. Claude reads them and never changes, renames or deletes them.

- Your CV. PDF is best. Save a Word file as PDF first.
- Optional: your LinkedIn profile, as a PDF or a data export (unzipped). START HERE.md says how to get it.
- Optional: anything else about your work, such as a performance review or a list of projects. One file per thing.
- A job ad you want to apply for, saved as a PDF. Claude uses ads to tailor your CV, never as facts about you.

It ships with files for Sam Whitlock, a made-up person, and a made-up job ad for Sam. Delete everything whose name starts with "Sam Whitlock" (three files and one folder) when you add your own.

This note is not about you. Claude skips it when it builds your career base.

Everything in this folder stays on your computer. The .gitignore file keeps it out of any git repo you make from this folder.
