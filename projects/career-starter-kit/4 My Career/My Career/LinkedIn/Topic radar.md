# Topic radar

The weekly scan writes here. Each signal is a piece of news from the last 7 to 10 days that touches your experience, with its link and a score out of 25 (see About LinkedIn). Signals are news about the world, never facts about you. If a week has nothing new, the scan writes one line saying so and adds no rows.

## What I scan for
Claude fills this from your Voice, Themes, Skills and Job Scout preferences the first time it scans. Edit it any time. Plain topics only, never your name or employers.
