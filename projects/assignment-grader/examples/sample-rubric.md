# Rubric: Business case essay (fictional sample)

Course: Introduction to Retail Strategy (fictional). Total 100 points.

| ID | Criterion | Max | Full marks looks like |
|----|-----------|-----|-----------------------|
| C1 | Argument | 30 | A clear position stated early and held through the essay, with counter-arguments answered. |
| C2 | Evidence | 25 | Claims backed by data or sources, with each figure explained. |
| C3 | Structure and clarity | 25 | Logical sections, one idea per paragraph, plain language. |
| C4 | Recommendation | 20 | A specific, costed next step with risks and a timeline. |

Bands: 85+ High Distinction, 75 to 84 Distinction, 65 to 74 Credit, 50 to 64 Pass, below 50 Fail.
