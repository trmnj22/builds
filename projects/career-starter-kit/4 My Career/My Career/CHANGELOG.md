# Changelog

## v3, Nov 2026
First shared version.

- One folder laid out like a Claude project or a git repo: README, CLAUDE.md, START HERE, this changelog and a .gitignore that keeps your files and notes out of any repo.
- Build my career base runs a quick build. Build my full career base runs the slower, fully checked build with a team of agents.
- Grill me asks the questions your CV leaves open and saves your answers into your notes and your Job Scout preferences.
- Write a LinkedIn post is now a full LinkedIn engine. It scans the last week's news on your topics into Topic radar.md, keeps a Topic backlog from your own career, offers three scored ideas (a fresh signal, a backlog topic and a counter-view), asks up to three questions, then drafts to a set post shape with a first comment, an evidence check and a humanizer pass. Your first post skips the scan and uses your own career only, so it is quicker. Scoring and the post shape are explained in LinkedIn/About LinkedIn.md.
- Fill it fills the LinkedIn composer in your own signed-in Chrome when Claude in Chrome is connected, and stops at the Post button. Nothing is ever posted for you.
- I posted it files the post with its link and adds its claims to Already said.md. Log my LinkedIn numbers saves day-7 numbers and keeps your own baseline.
- Set up my LinkedIn scan every Monday at 8am leaves three scored ideas in Ideas.md each week. It never drafts or posts.
- Grill me's LinkedIn questions now fill Voice.md and seed the Topic backlog.
- Ask my board of advisers gives you a panel of advisers who argue a decision with you.
- The humanizer skill tidies AI-sounding prose in CVs and posts. The other skills read its file directly, so a humanizer you already have installed does not replace it.
- Set up my Job Scout uses your grill-me answers when you have given them, so it asks less.
- Sam Whitlock's example files now include a LinkedIn data export.
- Sam's example job ad is now named "Sam Whitlock job ad - GM Customer and Operations.pdf", so deleting everything that starts with Sam Whitlock clears it too.
- The quick build writes fewer, shorter notes so a first run is quick. Grill me starts straight after it and has no limit. Answer as many as you like and click That's enough for now to stop.
