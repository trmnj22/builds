# About drafts

One note per LinkedIn draft, named with the date and a few words of the title. Each one has:
- **Post:** the text, exactly as it goes into LinkedIn.
- **First comment:** links and sources, to add as the first comment once the post is up.
- **Image:** a photo of your own that could go with it, or no image.
- **Evidence check:** every claim in the post and the note it came from.
- **Check before you post:** the last things to look at.

Read the check list and change anything that does not sound like you. Then copy the Post section into LinkedIn yourself, or say Fill it if Claude in Chrome is connected. Claude stops at the Post button. When it is up, type "I posted it".
