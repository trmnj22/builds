# Company context

**Who writes it:** a senior leader, once for the whole organisation.
**Who uses it:** everyone. It sits at the top of every team's and every person's AI setup.
**Length:** one page. Shorter is better.

> **Before you fill this in:** check your organisation's AI policy. Write only what you would be comfortable putting in a staff induction pack. No client names, no financials that aren't public, no personal information.

**Faster way:** paste this whole file into your AI tool and say: *"Interview me to fill this in. One question at a time, and suggest the answer you'd expect so I can say yes or correct you."*

---

## What we do
*In two or three sentences: who our customers are, what we give them, and how we make money.*

## Why it matters
*Our purpose, in plain words. What would be lost if we didn't exist?*

## Our values in practice
*Three to five. For each one, what it looks like in a real decision, not a poster.*
- Example: "Customer first" means we'll take a smaller margin to fix a customer's problem properly the first time.

## How we make decisions
*Who decides what, how much risk we take, and what we never trade away.*

## What AI must never do here
*The hard lines.*
- Example: never send anything to a customer without a person reading it first.
- Example: never use customer data in a tool the organisation hasn't approved.

## How we write
*Tone, spelling (for example Australian English), words we use and words we avoid.*

## What good looks like this year
*The two or three things the organisation is focused on in the next 12 months.*
