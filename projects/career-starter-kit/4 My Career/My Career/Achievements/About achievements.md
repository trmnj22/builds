# Achievements

One note per thing you delivered or changed, in your files' own words, with the exact numbers they give.
Each note links to its role and the skills it shows. If a result is missing, the note says gap.
The build writes these notes when you type "Build my career base".
You can edit any note here. End a line you change with "(told me" and the date, for example (told me 14 Nov 2026), and the next build keeps it.
