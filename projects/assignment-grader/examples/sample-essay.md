# Should Northwind Retail move its loyalty programme into an app?

Student: Alex Morgan (fictional sample)

Northwind Retail runs a plastic loyalty card across 40 stores. I argue it should move the programme into its existing shopping app within a year.

The main reason is reach. Northwind's own survey of 1,200 shoppers found that 62% already open the app at least once a month, while card scans at the till have fallen for three straight quarters. An app programme meets shoppers where they already are.

The second reason is data. A card tells Northwind what was bought. An app also tells it what was browsed and abandoned, which lets the marketing team send offers that match intent rather than history.

Some will say older shoppers prefer the card. That is fair, so the card should stay for two years as a fallback, linked to the same account.

Competitors such as Contoso Market have already moved, and their programme is popular.

Recommendation: Northwind should pilot the app programme in five stores next quarter, then roll it out if sign-ups pass 30% of active members.
