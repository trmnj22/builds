# Shortlists

The job scout writes one file here each day it runs, named `Shortlist YYYY-MM-DD.md`. A second run on the same day adds a new section to that day's file.

Shortlists hold real employer names from public job ads. They stay on your computer. Do not share the folder with shortlists in it unless you mean to.

Shortlists are research about jobs, never facts about you. The build never uses them as evidence.
